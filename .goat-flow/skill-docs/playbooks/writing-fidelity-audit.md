# Writing Fidelity Audit

Use this after the human-prose core for substantive recomposition or derivation with distinct claims, or when fidelity remains unresolved. It checks meaning against visible sources; it does not grant rewrite authority.

## Availability Check

This documentary workflow needs no CLI. The assessor must be able to inspect the source of truth and the proposed revision together. If the source or its relevant context is unavailable, stop the affected rewrite or fidelity judgment and name the missing evidence.

Routine spelling fixes, minimal corrections and compliant unchanged prose do not automatically require this audit. Derivation alone does not trigger it. Follow the core's correctness check unless fidelity remains unresolved.

## Project Authority

Project-documented fidelity policy governs source authority, permitted transformations and required evidence. When no designated project standard answers a question, use this playbook's defaults. Explicit current instructions and the authoritative project hierarchy remain higher. Project conventions and generic defaults cannot override safety, accepted architecture, verified facts, evidence requirements or verification gates.

Preserve the artifact's purpose, audience and authorised scope. A summary may omit detail; a correction may follow superior authority. State the basis for either instead of treating the original wording as immutable.

## Intent and Boundary

Make omissions, additions and changed claims checkable without confusing fluent prose with faithful prose. Judge fidelity separately from readability.

Keep evidence internal or in an already-required receipt. Reuse the existing selected-unit, changed-span and command-evidence ledgers; do not create another reporting framework or demand a detailed ledger from every sentence.

This method is guidance. Delivery and contract checks do not establish that agents follow it more reliably.

## Bind the Comparison

Identify the prose unit, its source revision or observed state, and the applicable source anchors before recomposing. Include the requested transformation and allowed omissions. Use file paths plus semantic anchors where files own the facts; distinguish current facts from proposals and historical observations.

Make both versions and the supporting context visible to the assessor. A judge given only the revision cannot establish source fidelity. Do not substitute memory, a score or another reviewer’s conclusion for the source.

## Compare in Both Directions

1. Read source to revision. Locate each distinct source claim that the task requires: what was retained, changed or omitted? Give an authorised summary omission its task or audience justification.
2. Read revision to source. Identify support for each added or changed claim. A new cause, condition, benefit, explanation or attribution needs supporting source evidence; task authority alone does not supply missing facts.
3. Check meaning dimensions. Preserve or justify the actor, action, number, identifier, scope, condition, qualifier, certainty, commitment and speech act. A suggestion, proposal and promise do different work.
4. Check claim strength in context. Changes such as can/will, might/would, little/no or some/every are leads to inspect, not automatic defects. Preserve the supported degree of certainty rather than requiring identical words.
5. Resolve each difference. Retain supported meaning, correct the draft, record an authorised omission or superior-authority correction, or defer the affected unit with its missing proof.

Exact wording, sentence order and detail level may change when the authorised meaning survives. Do not trade a concrete fact for a vaguer claim or add a restriction merely to make a sentence sound natural.

## Record the Result

For each modified qualifying prose unit, keep one source-linked result that covers its retained claims and any dropped, changed or added claims. Link to the existing unit and change records instead of duplicating them.

Use goat-clarity's existing `Meaning audit:` receipt field when that skill owns the work. Reconcile every modified qualifying human-prose unit once; use `none` when no qualifying meaning change needs an audit. Minimal edits and compliant unchanged prose do not gain compulsory detailed ledgers.

An authorised difference needs its source or task basis. An unresolved difference stays visible in the existing deferred or unverified outcome; it cannot be presented as a clean fidelity result.

## Verification

Before claiming the revision is faithful, confirm that:

- the compared sources and revision are the versions actually assessed;
- required source claims are retained or have supported reasons for change or omission;
- every added or changed claim has visible support;
- actors, conditions, qualifiers, commitments and certainty preserve the supported meaning; and
- unresolved evidence and the exact assessed scope remain visible separately from readability.

A source mismatch or unsupported meaning change stops the affected rewrite or judgment. Continue unrelated work only within its existing authority.

## Troubleshooting

When the prose needs a missing fact, name that fact and its owner only when the owner is known. Request the needed evidence or retain the existing incomplete outcome. Do not invent an answer, cause, permission or placeholder presented as fact.

If a short correction triggers this whole method merely because it uses a source, revisit the load condition. The detailed audit belongs to substantive recomposition or derivation with distinct claims, or unresolved fidelity.
